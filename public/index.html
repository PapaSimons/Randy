Play to Randy directly from the Spotify app on your phone or computer.

  1. Open the Spotify app on a phone or computer on the same network as this server.
  2. Start playing any track — the device list only appears during active playback.
  3. Open the devices menu and select SpotOn Authorization (Device Name).
